# Resume style

Perkins follows these rules whenever it writes a tailored resume. Edit them to taste.

- Two pages at most, about 1,100 words. Compress the oldest roles to one line each before cutting anything recent.
- No "I" in bullets. Short sentences. Lead each bullet with the result or the action.
- Numbers, titles, and dates exactly as the master resume states them.
- Mirror the posting's phrases only where they are true of your work.
- One new bullet per application that answers the posting's top requirement head-on, built only from facts in the master.

## Optional rules

Off until you move one up into the list above.

- No em dashes. Use commas, colons, periods, or parentheses instead.
- US spelling.

# Tailoring model

Every application gets a copy of the master resume with three things changed: the headline, the summary's opening, and the order of the evidence. The facts never change.

## The variants

Five example role types to start from. Rename the columns to the roles you target, and fill the cells from your own master resume. Ask Claude to draft them from your master if you'd like a first pass, then edit.

| | CTO | CIO | VP of agent engineering | AI transformation or AI product | Enterprise sales or account executive |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | Technology executive · Platform and engineering at scale | Technology and operations executive · Enterprise transformation | Agent engineering leader · Production agents with governance and telemetry | Transformation and product leader · From business process to production | Enterprise sales leader · From first deals to adoption at scale |
| Summary opens with | The platform shifts you led | The operating model you ran and its measured results | The agents you shipped to production and what they changed | A process you redesigned, end to end, with its results | Your selling record: quota attainment and landmark deals |
| Order after the summary | Signature results, hands-on, experience | Signature results, experience, a short hands-on | Hands-on first, then signature results, experience | Hands-on first, then signature results, experience | Signature results led by sales wins, then experience |
| Promote | Platform builds, architecture, the tools you chose | Savings, risk reduced, the metrics leadership tracked | Evaluation, guardrails, autonomy with human checkpoints | Process decomposition, adoption, the metrics that moved | New business, expansion, complex negotiations |
| Mirror their words for | Architecture, platform, roadmap, build versus buy | Governance, risk, operating model, vendors, change management | Orchestration, evaluation, observability, agent lifecycle | Human in the loop, product ownership, KPIs | New logos, expansion, executive engagement |
| Play down | Early sales roles | Code-level detail | Early sales roles | Unrelated early roles | Internal tooling detail |
| Gap to answer before they ask | No prior CTO title: answer with the scale of what you ran | Never owned corporate IT: answer with accountability you did own | Agents were low-code: answer with what you built yourself | Years in product titles: answer with measured product results | Years carrying a quota: answer with deals won and adoption |

One rule across all five: mirror a posting's words only where they are true of your work. A phrase you can't back in an interview costs more than it earns in a keyword scan.

## Per application, about 20 minutes

1. Get the job description: the posting link, or the referrer's copy. No description, no tailoring yet.
2. Pick the variant column above that fits the title and the top three requirements.
3. Swap in its headline and summary opening.
4. Mirror six to eight of the posting's exact phrases, only where they are true.
5. Reorder the sections and promote the bullets the column names. Keep it to two pages.
6. Write one bullet that answers the posting's top requirement head-on, from real work.
7. Review the draft and ask for edits. New facts go into the master resume too.
8. Apply yourself, then tell Perkins you sent it.
